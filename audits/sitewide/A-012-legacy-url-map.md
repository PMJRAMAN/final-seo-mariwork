## Mapping and identity contract

A redirect proves an observed transport destination, **not that the original product, variation, article or term is the same entity as that destination**. In particular, a volume-specific product slug may point at a current variable-product parent; parent/variation continuity needs historical WP/Woo records. No slug similarity was used to infer identity or choose a replacement. Historical entity equivalence remains UNKNOWN_NEEDS_VERIFICATION for redirected, canonical-alternate and 404 sources. No approved replacement map is asserted.

`destination_stable_entities` identifies an existing WP/term row for the current destination where available; `body_wp_ids` records the returned page ID independently. `destination_inventory_records` can also identify a URL-space record; that is coverage, not a stable entity identity. Source row membership from A-010 is retained as provenance, not endorsed as historical entity equivalence. Exact legacy source spellings and previous evidence are never erased.

For lookup only, requests-style URI encoding plus uppercase percent escapes reconcile literal Unicode and encoded equivalents; path case, slash and query order are retained. Encoding differences without a 3xx response are not redirects. The CSV stores the source spelling and actual response URL separately.

`initial_status` is the source response; `final_status` is the terminal response; the complete chain contains URL, status, target and X-Redirect-By at each hop. `canonical` is the list of link rel=canonical values from initial HTML at the terminal response, not Google’s selection. Canonical target checks reference fresh terminal responses where available; no canonical-only navigation was inferred as a redirect. HTTP Link canonicals, especially for binary files, were not evaluated. NOT_AVAILABLE means no HTML canonical found; missing/unrequested evidence is UNKNOWN_NEEDS_VERIFICATION. A 200 does not prove indexability, content usefulness or absence of a soft 404. JS/meta-refresh behavior and rendered content equivalence were not audited.

The five unrequested URLs have an empty recorded chain because no request was made; their safety limitation overrides any interpretation that they are direct responses. Historical metrics remain valid only for their export period, not evidence of current traffic loss or causation.
