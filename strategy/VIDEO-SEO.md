# Video SEO Strategy

**Status:** CORE / PLANNED
**Project rule:** ALL public site videos are in scope.

## Objective

Give every public Mariwork video an explicit SEO role, host-page relationship, discoverability state and QA outcome.

## Required Inventory Fields

For every video record:
- stable video identifier where available;
- host page/entity;
- page family;
- video topic/purpose;
- user/search intent;
- embed/player source;
- crawl/discovery evidence;
- title/description;
- thumbnail;
- surrounding text;
- transcript/summary availability where relevant;
- structured-data state;
- internal links;
- final disposition;
- QA state.

## Strategy Areas

- host-page indexability and canonical relationship;
- video prominence and contextual usefulness;
- title/description standards;
- thumbnail standards;
- VideoObject ownership where eligible;
- transcript/summary/key-text policy;
- Academy/Article/Product/Category cross-linking;
- duplicate or orphaned video handling;
- monitoring of video-search/discovery signals where available.

## Completion Rule

The program is not complete until every discovered public video is inventoried, reviewed, assigned a target state, implemented/kept as approved, and QA'd.
