# Mariwork Semantic Positioning & Product Value Pillars

**Status:** ACCEPTED FOUNDATION / EVIDENCE-BACKED EXPANSION ONLY  
**Primary authority:** DEC-121  
**Related decisions:** DEC-053, DEC-085, DEC-099, DEC-122 through DEC-125, DEC-129  
**Created:** 2026-09-26

## Why this document exists

This is the upstream content-strategy foundation for Mariwork.

Keyword research does not define what Mariwork is. Search demand helps prioritize and phrase already-valid semantic territory. Topic clusters, page ownership and content production must derive from this positioning rather than create a new positioning from keyword volume.

## Canonical entity baseline

Mariwork is an Iranian specialist brand/business that produces fabric colors and specialist products for painting and hand printing on fabric.

Important factual boundaries:
- canonical Persian name: `ماری‌ورک`;
- canonical English name: `Mariwork`;
- founder: `مریم مختاری` / `Maryam Mokhtari`;
- start year: 1395 SH;
- education is a genuine brand activity and one of Mariwork's main goals;
- Mariwork colors are for painting/printing **on fabric**, not fabric dyeing;
- Mariwork colors are fundamentally different from ordinary acrylic paints, but technical mechanism claims require evidence;
- not every item sold on mariwork.ir is Mariwork-manufactured/branded.

The canonical factual registry remains `strategy/BRAND-ENTITY-SEARCH.md`.

## Pillar 1 — Fabric suitability

### Strategic meaning
Mariwork should be strongly associated with the practical problem: choosing and using products designed for painting and hand printing on fabric.

### Safe baseline
- Products are positioned in the fabric-painting/hand-printing domain.
- Content may explain appropriate use cases when supported by real Product instructions and demonstrations.
- “Not dyeing” is a useful boundary.

### Evidence required before stronger claims
Specific fabric compatibility, wash behavior, fixation conditions, limitations or substrate-specific performance require Product instructions, documented testing or other verified evidence.

## Pillar 2 — Application quality

### Strategic meaning
Content should help users achieve a correct, controlled and useful application/result rather than merely describe a Product.

### Content territory
- preparation;
- application technique;
- layer/control guidance;
- printing/painting workflow;
- common mistakes;
- finish/result interpretation;
- tool/material selection.

### Claim boundary
Do not state measurable coverage, smoothness, adhesion, opacity, finish superiority or similar performance as fact unless evidence supports that exact claim.

## Pillar 3 — Durability / stability

### Strategic meaning
Durability/stability is an important user decision space and content pillar, especially around correct application, fixation/cure and care.

### Claim boundary
Do not turn this pillar into blanket claims such as “permanent”, “wash-proof”, “most durable” or fixed longevity numbers without verified evidence.

Content may explain the actual documented conditions required for durable results when the source is verified.

## Pillar 4 — Compatibility / system thinking

### Strategic meaning
Mariwork content should explain how colors, mediums, tools, techniques, fabrics and volumes relate when those relationships are real.

### Content territory
- which material/tool is used in a technique;
- which medium has which verified role;
- volume choice;
- Set/Bundle composition;
- relevant Product ↔ Academy handoffs;
- Product family compatibility.

### Claim boundary
Never infer compatibility merely because two Products are sold together or share a category.

## Pillar 5 — Economic value

### Strategic meaning
“Economic” does **not** mean “cheapest”.

The intended concept is value from evidence-backed useful performance and an appropriate purchase/use decision.

Possible value mechanisms, only when supported:
- choosing the appropriate volume for the task;
- avoiding unnecessary waste;
- reducing rework through correct technique/product choice;
- useful performance relative to the user's real task;
- Set/Bundle value where the actual commercial facts support it.

### Claim boundary
Do not publish unsupported savings percentages, cost-per-use superiority, “best value” or price superiority.

## Education — strategic content capability

Education is not merely another Product claim; it is a core Mariwork brand/content capability.

Academy owns video-first practical/demo instruction. Magazine owns text-first explanation/reference/editorial content.

Education should support the value pillars by showing correct use, evidence and practical decision support rather than acting as generic SEO filler.

## Evidence hierarchy for claims

Before a meaningful Product/brand claim is published, use the strongest appropriate evidence available:

1. verified Mariwork Product specification/instruction;
2. documented first-hand Mariwork test/demo with reproducible context;
3. reliable Woo/business/Product data;
4. credible external source for external technical/historical facts;
5. owner-confirmed brand facts for brand-history/entity statements.

Marketing attractiveness alone is not evidence.

Comparative/superiority and safety claims require especially strong support.

## Topic research workflow

For each pillar:
1. collect real user problems/questions;
2. inspect GSC Page+Query evidence where available;
3. perform current Search/SERP research;
4. group into intent/topic clusters rather than keyword variants;
5. prioritize by Mariwork relevance, evidence, user value and realistic opportunity;
6. assign one primary durable page-family owner;
7. design complementary journey coverage without duplication;
8. identify first-hand evidence that can make the content genuinely distinctive;
9. apply AI-answer/citation considerations from DEC-129 without creating thin GEO pages.

## What this foundation does not authorize

It does not authorize:
- unsupported technical claims;
- bulk content generation;
- doorway pages;
- keyword-stuffed archive intros;
- Product rewrites before evidence review;
- claiming a pillar as a measurable Product advantage without proof.
