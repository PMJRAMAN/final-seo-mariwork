# Brand / Entity Search Strategy

**Status:** CORE / PLANNED

## Objective

Create a consistent, evidence-backed representation of Mariwork across branded search, site content, structured data and external references.

## Scope

- branded queries and branded landing pages;
- Homepage;
- About / founder / history / contact / trust pages;
- Organization / WebSite / other appropriate entity schema;
- canonical brand name and factual attributes;
- consistent brand naming across titles/content;
- verifiable off-site references;
- interaction with external PR / advertorial campaigns.

## Required Outputs

1. brand/entity baseline;
2. branded-query map;
3. canonical brand facts registry;
4. entity-supporting content gap analysis;
5. schema/ownership target state;
6. off-site reference opportunities based on verifiable facts;
7. implementation plan;
8. branded-search monitoring plan.

## Safety / Accuracy Rule

Never invent awards, credentials, founders, partnerships, reviews, locations, dates or other brand/entity facts for SEO. Unsupported claims remain UNKNOWN_NEEDS_VERIFICATION.


## DEC-053 Working Canonical Facts Registry

**Status:** OWNER-CONFIRMED WORKING FACTS / DEC-053 NOT YET ACCEPTED

These facts were confirmed directly by the owner during DEC-053 discussion. They are the factual baseline for later brand/entity decisions. Do not expand them into stronger claims without separate evidence.

### Brand identity and naming

- Canonical Persian brand name: `ماری‌ورک`.
- Do not use `ماری ورک` or `ماریورک` as the standard Persian brand spelling.
- Canonical English brand name: `Mariwork`.
- Standard English brand spelling is `Mariwork`; do not use `Mari Work`, `MARIWORK` or `mariwork` as ordinary brand prose except where a technical or design constraint requires it.
- Official website: `mariwork.ir`.
- Mariwork is an Iranian brand/business. Do not describe it by default as a company or legal entity unless a verified legal name is specifically required.
- The brand may be referred to simply as `ماری‌ورک`. `رنگ پارچه ماری‌ورک` is a descriptive/product-context phrase, not the formal brand name, and must not be mechanically injected into every Title, H1 or body text.
- Mariwork operates specifically in the field of fabric painting and hand printing.

### Founder and history

- Founder: `مریم مختاری`.
- Canonical English founder name: `Maryam Mokhtari`.
- Canonical founder title: Founder of Mariwork. Do not add other professional titles without separate verification.
- Start year: 1395 SH.
- Owner-confirmed experience claim: more than 10 years.
- The exact start month is intentionally not recorded; do not derive or publish a precise Gregorian founding anniversary from the year alone.

### Brand definition and activity

- Full brand definition: Mariwork produces fabric colors and supplies for hand printing and painting on fabric.
- Preferred concise brand definition: `ماری‌ورک تولیدکننده رنگ‌های پارچه و محصولات تخصصی نقاشی و چاپ روی پارچه است.`
- Mariwork's fabric colors are designed for painting and printing on fabric, not for fabric dyeing.
- Mariwork fabric colors must not be described as ordinary acrylic paint or as merely a subtype/equivalent of standard acrylic paint; the owner confirms fundamental differences in product nature and use. Specific technical mechanisms require separate evidence before publication.
- Mariwork is an Iranian specialist brand in fabric colors and products for fabric painting and printing.
- Products are formulated in Iran by Iranian engineers using foreign-origin materials.
- Production, mixing, filling and final preparation are performed in Iran.
- Primary market/activity geography: Iran. Do not imply international market presence by default.

### Product ownership and portfolio

- Mariwork manufactures its core product portfolio, including fabric colors, mediums and other specialist products.
- The portfolio includes fabric colors, mediums, glitters, shine products, sets, tools/supplies and other specialist products related to fabric painting and printing.
- A small number of products in the `Other Tools` area are third-party products; the owner currently estimates fewer than about 3–4 items.
- A product being sold on mariwork.ir does not by itself make it Mariwork-branded or Mariwork-manufactured.
- Third-party products must not be represented in titles, content, schema or product identity as Mariwork-branded/manufactured.

### Audience and education

- Primary audience: artists, learners/students, and people working professionally or educationally in fabric painting and hand printing.
- Mariwork is active in education as well as product manufacturing/sales.
- Developing the art through education is one of Mariwork's main brand goals.
- Mariwork must not be reduced to or described solely as an educational institute.

- Mariwork products are also available through physical stores that stock/sell Mariwork products.
- Canonical wording for those third-party physical outlets: `فروشگاه‌های عرضه‌کننده محصولات ماری‌ورک`.
- Mariwork currently has no official or exclusive representative/dealer network.
- Do not call these outlets official representatives, exclusive representatives, authorized dealers, branches, or Mariwork-owned stores unless a specific location is separately verified to have that status.
- Exact store/location/NAP treatment remains governed by DEC-087.
