## Exact target state

- Homepage = brand/entity gateway + top-level navigation.
- Academy = video-first practical/demo/instructional content.
- Magazine/Articles = text-first explanatory/reference/editorial content.
- Magazine hub and durable Blog Categories are editorial discovery hubs.
- Static/trust pages remain purpose-specific; no generic SEO filler.
- About/Why/Contact/Stores/FAQ use only verified facts.
- current Articles use Mariwork Organization authorship unless a real identified author is approved.
- FAQ is visible/useful content first; no artificial FAQ for schema.
- Stores represent third-party stockists/sellers, not branches/official representatives by default.
- content overlap is resolved by intent evidence, not topic similarity alone.
- DEC-121 semantic positioning is upstream of keyword/topic expansion.
- cluster research → one primary page-family owner → coherent content journey.
- Product/brand claims require claim-appropriate evidence.
- every durable indexable archive/hub gets a concise unique useful content block.
